Northern suburbs NDIS support

NDIS Support Worker in Melbourne’s Northern Suburbs

Rush Connect & Care provides smaller-scale one-to-one NDIS support across Melbourne’s inner and northern suburbs. RCC currently operates as an unregistered provider, so this page is for participants, families, plan managers and support coordinators looking for practical, steady and relationship-based support where an unregistered provider is suitable for the participant’s funding arrangement.

Check local availability See transparent pricing

Suburbs and nearby areas

Support may be suitable across areas such as Preston, Reservoir, Thornbury, Northcote, Coburg, Brunswick, Heidelberg, Bundoora, Ivanhoe, Epping, Greensborough, Alphington, Fairfield and nearby Melbourne suburbs where travel and timing are realistic. The best way to check fit is to include the participant’s suburb, support goals and preferred days in the enquiry.

For participants

Support can help with getting out of the house, transport, appointments, shopping, social connection, routine and practical confidence.

For families and carers

A smaller-provider feel can make support less overwhelming when the participant benefits from consistency, trust and clear communication.

For referrers

Support coordinators and plan managers can check pricing, resources, scope and availability before referring ongoing support.

Why local fit matters

NDIS support works better when the support is realistic, consistent and connected to the person’s actual life. Local knowledge helps with transport planning, community access, familiar activities, appointment support and building routine around places the participant can return to over time.

Start with a short local enquiry

Include the participant’s suburb, funding type, broad goals, preferred times and whether the need is community access, routine, overnight reassurance, transport, social support or another practical support area. If the plan is NDIA-managed, please check whether a registered provider is required before enquiring.
