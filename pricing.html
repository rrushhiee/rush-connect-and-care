Important details

Before support starts

Pricing should be checked against the participant’s current NDIS plan, goals, funding type, and any plan manager requirements before ongoing support begins.

Current NDIS-capped rates are checked against the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits.

Rush Connect & Care is not crisis support, emergency response, clinical therapy, medical care, or behaviour support. Support is practical, one-to-one, and delivered within clear boundaries.
