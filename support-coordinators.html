Referral-Ready NDIS Support in Melbourne

This page is built for support coordinators, plan managers, allied health referrers, NDIS service providers and families who need to quickly check whether Rush Connect & Care is the right fit for a participant. RCC provides smaller-scale one-to-one NDIS support across Melbourne, with a focus on practical support, clear communication, participant choice and transparent pricing.

Rush Connect & Care currently operates as an unregistered NDIS provider. The referral pathway is best suited to self-managed and plan-managed participants where the participant’s funding arrangement allows an unregistered provider and the support need is practical, non-clinical and within clear boundaries.

When RCC may be a good referral option

Rush Connect & Care may be a good option for participants who benefit from consistency, smaller-provider communication and practical one-to-one support. This can include community access, routine building, appointments, transport, motivation, social connection, overnight reassurance, confidence building and practical goal follow-through. The service may also suit participants who prefer a steady male support worker or a lived-experience approach where that fit is appropriate.

Information referrers usually need quickly

  • Service area: Melbourne, with a focus on the inner and northern suburbs
  • Funding fit: self-managed and plan-managed participants where support aligns with the participant’s plan, goals and ability to use an unregistered provider
  • Registration status: unregistered NDIS provider, not suitable for every funding pathway
  • Service boundaries: not crisis support, emergency response, therapy, behaviour support or medical care
  • Trust markers: insurance, NDIS Worker Screening, Working With Children Check, First Aid and clear communication
  • Next step: send a short enquiry or referral sheet with goals, suburb, preferred times and support need

Referral pathway

  1. Send a referral enquiry or referral sheet.
  2. RCC checks service fit, location, availability and boundaries.
  3. Participant, family and referrer confirm the support goal and practical details.
  4. Service agreement and pricing are confirmed before support begins.
