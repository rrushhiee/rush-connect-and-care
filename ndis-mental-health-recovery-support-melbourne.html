NDIS mental health support Melbourne

NDIS Mental Health & Recovery Support in Melbourne.

Consistent, peer support focused on independence, routine, and long-term recovery.

Rush Connect & Care provides practical, non-clinical one-to-one NDIS support for people who benefit from steady routines, community access, lived understanding, and clear support coordination.

Start an NDIS enquiry For support coordinators

Who this is for

Support for participants and referrers

Participants

Support for people working on independence, confidence, structure, daily routines, appointments, social connection, and community access.

Support coordinators

A referral-ready option for participants who may benefit from consistent one-to-one support, clear communication, practical documentation, and stable follow-through.

Families and carers

A steady support relationship that can help reduce isolation, build routine, and encourage meaningful next steps while keeping boundaries clear.

Support focus

Practical recovery support

  • Building and maintaining daily routines
  • Community access, social participation, and confidence outside the home
  • Support to attend appointments, activities, study, work, or community commitments
  • Motivation, planning, and small achievable weekly goals
  • Household routine support where it fits the participant’s plan and goals
  • Consistent peer support from someone who understands mental health recovery from lived experience

Why it can help

Consistency matters in recovery

Routine and momentum

Support is built around realistic steps that can be repeated over time, rather than pressure to change everything at once.

Relatable peer support

Lived experience can help support feel less clinical, more human, and easier to engage with when motivation or trust is low.

Clear communication

Participants, families, support coordinators, and plan managers can expect simple updates, honest boundaries, and practical follow-through.

Referral ready

Why Rush Connect & Care?

  • Checks and credentials include a Working With Children Check, NDIS Worker Screening, NDIS training, Victoria Police Check, and necessary insurance.
  • Clear boundaries are part of the service: Rush Connect & Care is not crisis support, therapy, emergency response, behaviour support, or medical care.
  • Support is practical, one-to-one, with communication kept clear for participants, families, plan managers, and support coordinators.
  • Built as a small, one-to-one support service, Rush Connect & Care prioritises consistency, trust, and real-world outcomes over high-volume service delivery.

How to start

A simple enquiry pathway

1

Send a short enquiry

Share the participant’s goals, suburb, funding type, and what kind of support is needed.

2

Check fit and availability

We can confirm whether the support request fits the service scope, location, and current availability.

3

Agree next steps

If it is a fit, the next step is service agreement, scheduling, and practical support planning.

Important: Rush Connect & Care provides non-clinical support. It is not crisis support, emergency care, clinical therapy, behaviour support, or medical care. The focus is reliable, high-quality practical support that helps participants work toward meaningful outcomes. If there is immediate risk, call emergency services or a crisis support service.

Enquiries welcome

Start with a short message

A few details are enough to begin: what support is needed, where the participant is based, and whether the plan is self-managed or plan-managed.
