Support Now

On-demand support workers, built around real life

Support Now is a Rush Connect & Care service pathway for people who may need practical company-style support, community access, transport, youth mentoring, or overnight reassurance without the large-provider feel.

The long-term vision is simple: make safe, suitable support easier to request, closer to calling on a trusted steady person when life needs a bit of backup.

Current stage

Prototype and waitlist

This is not yet a live instant-dispatch marketplace. It is the first public version for testing demand, collecting support requests, and finding suitable workers.

Early access

How it will work

A simple support request flow

1

Tell us what support is needed

Company, transport, errands, community access, youth mentoring, routine support, or overnight reassurance.

2

Check safety and fit

Support type, urgency, risk, funding, location, worker suitability, and boundaries are checked before matching.

3

Match with a suitable person

The aim is a practical match: someone calm, reliable, nearby, and appropriate for the person and support need.

4

Confirm and follow up

Details, pricing, boundaries, and next steps are confirmed before support begins, with feedback after support.

Support types

What people may request

Company and community access

A steady person for walks, errands, appointments, groceries, coffee, social connection, or getting out of the house.

Youth mentoring

Support that feels relaxed and practical for young people who may respond better to trust, activity, and conversation.

Overnight reassurance

Sleepover-style support where an appropriate worker provides a calm presence so someone feels less alone overnight.

The future version may include live availability, location-based matching, worker profiles, request tracking, payment handling, and ratings. The first version is deliberately simpler: collect the right information, protect safety, test demand, and build a worker network before adding automation.

Support Now is not crisis support, emergency response, clinical therapy, medical care, behaviour support, or a replacement for urgent services. NDIS-related support must also fit the participant, plan, funding type, and lawful support rules.
