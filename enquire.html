Enquire about youth mentoring or NDIS support

This form is for online youth mentoring, parent or carer enquiries, and NDIS support. Rush Connect & Care is predominantly male-focused where that fit helps, but enquiries are welcome for young people and participants of any gender. If you are looking for support for a young person who feels lost, is acting out, or is struggling with the pressure of becoming an adult, a few short details are enough to start.

Enquiry form

Your enquiry is sent to hello@rushconnectandcare.com.au. Required fields are marked by the form. If you would rather speak first, call 0423 815 267.

What happens next

  • Your message is sent directly to Rupert at Rush Connect & Care.
  • You will receive a reply using the email address you provide.
  • If it sounds like the right fit, the next step is a simple first conversation.

Support overview

Parents and carers

You do not need to have everything worked out before making contact.

A short message about what is going on is enough to get started.

Online youth mentoring

Mentoring sessions are delivered online so support can be easier to access consistently.

NDIS support

NDIS support is treated separately from youth mentoring and can be discussed when practical one-to-one support is the right fit.

Rush Connect & Care

Our longer term vision is to connect people who need support with support workers as quickly as possible.
